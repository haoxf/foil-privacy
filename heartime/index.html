HearTime 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 31 日

English简体中文

HearTime（以下简称“本 App”）是一款语音计时工具。本政策说明本 App 如何处理您的数据。

1. 本地数据

您创建的预设、流程、计时偏好、声音与显示设置，以及正在进行的计时恢复信息保存在您的设备上。本 App 不运营账户系统或独立数据服务器，不会将这些数据用于广告、营销、画像或跨 App 追踪。

2. 语音与系统服务

本 App 使用 iOS 提供的语音、音频、通知、Live Activity 和系统邮件服务来实现您选择的功能。语音内容在设备上处理；本 App 不将您的计时内容或音频录音上传给开发者。

3. 崩溃诊断与反馈

本 App 会在设备上本地保存最近不超过 3 份崩溃诊断记录，以帮助您主动报告问题。这些记录最长保留至被新的记录替换；本 App 不会在后台自动将它们上传给开发者。

只有当您主动选择“发送反馈”时，本 App 才会生成一封预填环境信息的邮件草稿。您可在邮件应用中查看、修改或取消发送。诊断信息可能包含 App 版本、系统版本、设备型号、线程、已加载映像路径、内存地址和错误信息；不会故意包含您创建的预设或流程内容。

发送至支持邮箱的邮件、邮件地址及您选择发送的附件只用于支持和故障排查，不用于营销或追踪；通常会在问题解决后 90 天内删除，法律、安全或争议处理需要时可能保留更长时间。

4. 系统权限

通知仅在您授权后用于计时完成提醒。Live Activity 和后台音频仅用于展示或播放您主动开始的计时。您可随时在系统设置中修改这些权限。

5. 第三方服务与安全

本 App 不集成第三方广告网络或数据经纪服务。我们依赖 Apple 平台的应用沙盒和系统安全机制保护本地数据；任何存储或传输方式都无法保证绝对安全。

6. 政策更新与联系

数据处理方式发生重大变化时，我们会更新本政策与生效日期。隐私、数据删除或支持问题请联系：andping@126.com。

HearTime Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

English简体中文

HearTime (the “App”) is a voice-guided timer. This policy explains how the App handles your data.

1. Local data

Presets, routines, timer preferences, voice and display settings, and limited timer-resume information are stored on your device. The App does not operate an account system or independent data server, and does not use this data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

2. Speech and system services

The App uses iOS speech, audio, notification, Live Activity, and system-mail services for features you choose to use. Speech is processed on the device. The App does not upload your timer content or audio recordings to the developer.

3. Crash diagnostics and feedback

The App may keep no more than three crash diagnostic records locally on your device so that you can choose to report a problem. They are retained only until replaced by newer records. The App never uploads them to the developer automatically in the background.

A prefilled email draft is created only when you actively choose Send Feedback. You can review, edit, or cancel it in your mail app. Diagnostics may include App and system versions, device model, threads, loaded-image paths, memory addresses, and error details; the App does not intentionally include presets or routine content.

Messages, email addresses, and attachments you choose to send are used only for support and troubleshooting, not for marketing or tracking. They are generally deleted within 90 days after resolution, unless longer retention is necessary for legal, security, or dispute-resolution purposes.

4. System permissions

Notifications are used only with your permission for timer-completion alerts. Live Activities and background audio are used only to display or play a timer you actively start. You can change permissions in system settings at any time.

5. Third-party services and security

The App does not integrate third-party advertising networks or data brokers. We rely on Apple platform protections, including app sandboxing and system security, to protect local data; no storage or transmission method can be completely secure.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy and its effective date when data practices materially change. For privacy, deletion, or support questions, contact andping@126.com.
